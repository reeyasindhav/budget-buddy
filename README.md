Design Ideas: Clean, minimal finance UI, expense category pie charts, monthly budget progress bars, savings goal tracker, transaction history cards.

Problem Statement:

Many individuals struggle to manage their personal finances effectively due to a lack of simple, centralised tools to monitor their daily cash flow. Without a clear overview of recurring income and category- wise expenditures, people often overspend, fail to meet their monthly savings targets, and find it difficult to assess their overall financial health. Budgetly addresses this problem by providing an intuitive, minimal platform that consolidates income and expense tracking, visualises spending patterns through clean charts, and helps users set and achieve actionable savings goals in real time.
